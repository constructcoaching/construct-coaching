The curriculum

Four sessions.
Everything you need.

Each session is 60 minutes, 1:1 with me over Google Meet. I tailor every session to your background and target role. Start with just Module 1 and decide from there — or book the full program upfront.

I failed at my first sales job. Almost failed my next one. I had no idea what the job actually entailed, how interviews worked, or how to position my background. This program fixes all of that.

Module 01

Why sales?

This isn't a lecture — it's a conversation. I share my story: the pivot, the failure, what sales actually gave me. Then we flip it to you. Where are you coming from, what's pulling you toward sales, and do you actually know what you're signing up for? We figure that out together before you spend a dollar more.

60 min · 1:1 $100 · credited
Module 02

What is the job?

The session I needed before my first attempt. Day in the life of an SDR and AE, how sales teams are structured, what the tech stack looks like (CRM, sequencing tools, dialers), what cold outbound actually means in practice. You leave knowing what the job is before you interview for it.

60 min · 1:1 Reality check
Module 03

Finding the role + CV alignment.

How to search for sales roles intelligently, how to read a job posting for what they're really looking for, and the critical work: bridging your existing background to what the role demands. The accountant who managed client relationships. The teacher who ran 30-person classrooms. We turn your history into a sales story.

60 min · 1:1 Deliverable: new CV
Module 04

Interview prep.

How a sales interview process is structured — recruiter screen, hiring manager, mock pitch. What each stage is actually testing. How to carry yourself like someone who already understands the sales mindset. You'll leave with a clear plan for how to show up at each stage.

60 min · 1:1 Stage-by-stage playbook
