How it works

A method,
not a script.

Every conversation runs through the same three beats. Same structure, every time, regardless of whether we're talking about an interview or a difficult talk about your finances.

i.

Find the root

Most "stuck" isn't really stuck — it's looking at the wrong layer. We figure out what the actual problem is before trying to solve it.

ii.

Name it plainly

I'll say what I see. No softening, no empty validation. Honest is more useful than nice — and it's what most people aren't getting elsewhere.

iii.

One real next step

Every session ends with one specific thing to do before the next. Small enough to actually do. Meaningful enough to matter.
