What we work on

The harder stuff —
and why I can actually speak to it.

These sessions are open-ended by design. Most people come in with one thing and realize there's something else underneath it. That's fine. We'll go where the conversation needs to go.

01 · Body & health

Weight & body image.

I was problematically overweight through most of my twenties. Never exercised, pre-diabetic, terrible body image. Then I turned it around — and learned that the mental side is the part nobody talks about enough.

02 · Money

Debt & financial management.

I've helped people close to me build real budgets, chip away at debt, and start actually saving — setting them on a path to a financial future that doesn't feel hopeless. Practical, direct, no judgment about where you're starting from.

03 · Direction

Career & purpose.

I left a stable CPA career to pivot into tech sales — and failed at it the first time. I know what it feels like to start over without knowing if it'll work. I also know what it feels like when it does.
